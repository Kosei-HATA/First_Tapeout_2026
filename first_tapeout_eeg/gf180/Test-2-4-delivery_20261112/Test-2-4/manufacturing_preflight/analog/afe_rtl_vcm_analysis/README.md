## What is established
VCM is fed by two nominal 100 kohm poly resistors. No explicit external VCM capacitor is connected in this bench. The specification comment intends off-chip decoupling but gives no value here. CMFB senses VCM; switched CPF and disabled offset-DAC plate circuitry also connect to it. Sixteen parallel RIN wrappers per output return to VCM. EP/EN are ideal dependent voltage sources between each electrode node and VCM, so input common-mode tracks VCM exactly (maximum numerical mismatch 6.7e-16 V), and their return currents join VCM. This differs from a specified real electrode/reference impedance network.

The approximately 94.9 mV peak-to-peak VCM ripple is synchronous with input-chopper/offset phase transitions: minimum is 1.74 ns after CIA rise / 1.32 ns after OFPH rise; maximum is 6.46 ns after CIA fall, approximately 0.40 ns before OFPH falling midpoint. Local cycle ripple RMS is about 2.13 mV; peak-to-peak alone overstates its sustained size. These are waveform correlations, not proof that any single branch causes all ripple. Branch currents were not saved, and nearby events share the same digital clock.

VCLK minimum 2.805756 V is 0.77 ns after CIA rise / 0.35 ns after OFPH rise. It is consistent with switching current drawn through the finite positive feed resistor and local decap. The bench has common ideal AVSS and no finite substrate/package network. Do not label this result whole-chip supply integrity signoff. Both VCM and VCLK can affect switch timing and common-mode behavior; no quantified EEG-band or ENOB impact has yet been established.

## External-capacitor and model limits
A capacitor would lower high-frequency VCM impedance but also lengthen midpoint startup and add ESR/ESL-dependent behavior. The simple divider approximation gives Rth approximately 50 kohm and tau approximately 50 kohm * Cext (1 nF -> 50 us; 100 nF -> 5 ms), before active/bench loads. These are examples, not selected board values. Adding a large ideal capacitor without extending settling or modeling its connection cannot establish correct startup. Existing Q representation preserves the integrated official C(V) relation but changes internal numerical realization and omits the original regularization; earlier resistor comparison was only a limited startup check. Official full-AFE C-form transient did not complete, so no measured official-model ripple equivalence exists.
